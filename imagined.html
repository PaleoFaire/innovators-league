How this page works

The four statuses

A status judges the idea as the story describes it, anywhere in the world, not the companies listed under it. Each one cites a dated fact from a primary source: an agency, a regulator, a company's own announcement or a peer-reviewed paper.

Where the stories come from

Each idea links to one early appearance in Not Boring's Sci-Fi Idea Bank, which builds on Bill Christensen's Technovelgy. It is one early appearance, not always the first.

How companies are matched

Each idea has written rules that pick companies from this database by sector, tags and technical approach. We checked every list by hand and the lists refresh daily, so new companies that fit a rule appear on their own.

The rules and sources are open: themes.json and the method. See something wrong? Tell us.
