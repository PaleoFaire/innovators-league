# Pre-registered studies — The Build-Out Pulse

Registered 26 September 2026, before the evaluation data exist. Definitions below are frozen; results are published whatever they show. If a definition has to change, the change and its reason are logged in CHANGELOG.md and the study restarts its clock.

## Study 1 — Does hiring precede raising?

- **Hypothesis.** Companies that record a capital event in month M or M+1 have higher three-month role growth to M than companies that do not.
- **Population.** Companies on the constant hiring panel with ≥5 open roles at M−3, in-lane, private, active.
- **Measure.** Median three-month role growth; share with growth ≥30%; share with negative growth. Raisers vs non-raisers.
- **Evaluation.** Reported every month in `studies/hiring_before_raise.md`. **Judged on the 1 April 2027 print**, when the panel will have been wide for six months. Pre-committed success criterion: raisers' median growth exceeds non-raisers' by ≥5 points AND raisers' share-down is lower, with n(raisers) ≥ 40.
- **Failure is published** with the same prominence.

## Study 2 — Does the Pulse lead the Build-Out Index?

- **Hypothesis.** The change in a bucket's hiring diffusion over three months predicts the sign of that bucket's listed-supplier basket return over the following quarter.
- **Population.** Buckets with a sufficient panel (≥20) in the month of prediction; the corresponding bucket of the Build-Out Index (Supplier Map v1 mapping from the October 2026 calls).
- **Measure.** Hit rate of sign prediction; Spearman correlation between diffusion change and next-quarter basket return.
- **Evaluation.** Quarterly from the January 2027 print; **judged on the 1 October 2027 print** (four quarters). Pre-committed criterion: hit rate > 60% across ≥ 20 bucket-quarters.

## Study 3 — Does the factory-coming flag precede a facility announcement?

- **Hypothesis.** Companies flagged (a senior manufacturing / plant / facilities hire posted) announce a new facility, lease or groundbreaking within nine months at a higher rate than unflagged companies of the same bucket.
- **Population.** All flagged companies from the October 2026 print onward; matched unflagged companies by bucket and panel month.
- **Measure.** Share with a confirmed footprint event (footprint.json) within nine months.
- **Evaluation.** Judged on the **1 October 2027 print**. Pre-committed criterion: flagged rate ≥ 2× unflagged, with ≥ 30 flagged companies.
