# The Pulse Note — September 2026

*The Build-Out Pulse, first Note. Nowcast to 26 September; the month-end value prints on 1 October. Shadow edition: for the Circle and the design partners, not yet the public page.*

## The finding

**American hard tech is hiring builders faster than coders, and the gap is widening every month.** Across the constant panel of private companies we track, manufacturing, technician and production roles have gone from 490 to 818 since April; software, data and product roles from 433 to 521. The ratio is 1.57, up from 1.13, and it has risen in each of the last five prints. Whatever the discourse says about AI eating engineering, the companies building reactors, rockets, drones and factories are posting for welders, machinists and plant managers.

**The Pulse is 63.2 — expansion, up from 59.6 in August.** Twenty-six of the fifty-three companies on the panel added roles this month; twelve cut. Open roles are 3,328, up 10% on the month and 47% since April. Breadth dipped through the summer (67 in April, 59 in June) and has now recovered; the total never stopped rising.

**Money is arriving; new government awards are thinning.** On the covered panel, 45 of every hundred companies with a funding history raised in the last three months — steady since April. Federal-award breadth has fallen from 57 to 42 over the same period. Read together: the private cohort is being financed by capital, not by contracts, and the award pipeline is the number to watch into the January budget season.

**Seven companies posted the hire that comes before a factory.** Chaos Industries, ClearSpace, Divergent, General Matter, Mach Industries, Muon Space and Nuro each opened a senior manufacturing, plant or facilities role this month. Historically that role precedes the facility announcement by months.

**The mover is Mach Industries: 20 open roles to 128 in a month**, the week of a capital event on our record. Astranis went 86 to 166; Divergent 70 to 112.

**For the nuclear issue:** the seven reactor developers on the panel went from 222 open roles in April to 386 now — up 74% across the summer of criticalities. Rung 2 is hiring like rung 4.

## Does hiring precede raising?

First cut, seven months of data: companies that raised in the month or the next had median three-month role growth of 20.6% against 14.3% for the rest, and only 12.5% of them had cut roles versus 28.1%. Eight raisers — not evidence, a direction. It re-runs every month, and if it fails, that prints too.
