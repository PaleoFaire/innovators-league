# The Pulse Note — September 2026

*The Build-Out Pulse, first Note. Method v1.2 (US-only universe, per-posting counts). Nowcast to 26 September; the month-end value prints on 1 October. Shadow edition: for the Circle and the design partners, not yet the public page.*

## The finding

**American hard tech is hiring builders faster than coders, and the gap widens every month.** Across the constant panel of private US companies we track, manufacturing and hardware-engineering roles have gone from 1,047 to 1,776 since April; software roles from 421 to 527. The ratio is 3.37, up from 2.49, and it has risen in each of the five prints since April. Whatever the discourse says about AI eating engineering, the companies building reactors, rockets, drones and factories are posting for mechanical engineers, technicians and plant managers three to one over software.

**The Pulse is 61.5 — expansion, slightly down from 62.8 in August.** The 90% interval is 52.1–70.8. Twenty-three of the forty-eight companies on the panel added roles this month; thirteen held; twelve cut. Open roles are 3,141, up 10% on the month and **52% since April**. Breadth has held between 61 and 68 all summer while the total has risen every single month — the expansion is broad and it is not slowing.

**Money is arriving; new government awards are thinning.** Capital breadth on the covered panel is 41 (155 companies with a funding history; 39 raised in the last three months). Federal-award breadth has fallen from 57 in April to 42. Read together: the private cohort is being financed by capital, not by contracts, and the award pipeline is the number to watch into the January budget season.

**Six companies posted the hire that comes before a factory.** Chaos Industries, Divergent, General Matter, Mach Industries, Muon Space and Nuro each opened a senior manufacturing, plant or facilities role this month. That role precedes the facility announcement by months.

**The mover is Mach Industries: 20 open roles to 128 in a month**, alongside a capital event on our record. Astranis went 86 to 166; Divergent 70 to 112.

**For the nuclear issue:** the five US reactor developers on the panel went from 194 open roles in April to 348 now — up 79% across the summer of criticalities, diffusion 70. Rung 2 is hiring like rung 4.

## Does hiring precede raising?

First cut, seven months of data: companies that raised in the month or the next had median three-month role growth of 23.4% against 15.1% for the rest, and only 14% of them had cut roles versus 30%. Seven raisers — not evidence, a direction. It re-runs every month against the pre-registered definition; if it fails, that prints too.

## What changed in the method this week — and what it did to the numbers

The universe is now **US-headquartered only** (716 companies of 1,208 tracked; six European and one New Zealand company left the panel), each posting counts once rather than each title, and a board-change guard holds out feed failures. Every month was recomputed and every change is logged: April 67.0 → 63.3, May 66.0 → 67.8, June 58.8 → 63.0, July 60.6 → 60.9, August 59.6 → 62.8. The direction of every finding survived; the levels moved by up to four points. That is what the revisions log is for.
