# Who's Building It: method

Generated by `scripts/build_imagined.py` from its constants. Do not edit by hand.

Method version 1.0. 27 ideas, 300 companies. Statuses last checked 2026-11-25.

## What the page shows

Each idea pairs one early appearance in science fiction with where the technology stands today and the companies in this database working on it. The fiction entry is one early appearance from Not Boring's Sci-Fi Idea Bank (https://notboring.com/sci-fi-idea-bank), not always the first. Not Boring's Sci-Fi Idea Bank builds on Bill Christensen's Technovelgy. We link to its entries and do not copy their text.

## The status ladder

- **Built**: People use it for its main purpose outside a test: paying customers, routine operations or a public service.
- **Demonstrated**: It has worked in a real test, but nobody uses it routinely yet.
- **In development**: Teams are building it and parts of it work, but the whole thing has not worked yet.
- **Still fiction**: Nobody has shown it working, even in a test.

The status judges the idea as the story describes it, anywhere in the world, not the companies listed under it. Every status carries one dated fact from a primary source (an agency, a regulator, a company's own newsroom or a peer-reviewed paper) and one sentence on what is still missing. The Idea Bank is never the evidence. A status without a dated source is refused by the build, and so is a source dated after the build date. A primary page that prints no date is cited as undated, together with the day we read it.

## How companies are matched

Each idea has rules in `data/imagined/themes.json`: database subsectors, tags, and a pattern read against the company name, subsector, tags and technical approach (descriptions only where a rule says `wide`). The lists were then checked by hand, with corrections kept in `include` and `exclude`. Dead and acquired companies drop out. The daily sync rebuilds the lists from that day's data.js, so new companies that fit a rule appear on their own. Names in `include` or `exclude` that leave the database are reported in the build log, never guessed.

Within an idea, companies are ordered by total raised, largest first, then by name.

## House style

Titles, facts and gap lines are plain sentences. Length limits: title 48 characters, imagined 160 characters, fact 240 characters, gap 200 characters, publisher 80 characters. No em dashes.

## Rebuild

```
python3 scripts/build_imagined.py --check    # validate the theme file
python3 scripts/build_imagined.py            # write data/imagined_auto.js
python3 -m unittest scripts/tests/test_imagined.py
node scripts/tests/test_imagined_page.js
```
